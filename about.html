About

An engineer who cares what happens after the code runs.

Professional background

I am Mercy Kathomi Mbaka, a Kenya-based software engineer and founder. I design, build, integrate and deploy software for institutions and real operational workflows, with a focus on backend systems: Python, Django, PostgreSQL, APIs, authentication, payment integrations and deployment.

Career journey

Software Engineer Attaché, Survey of Kenya / Ministry of Lands, September to December 2025. This was an attachment (internship) involving digital workflow implementation and ICT monitoring, with exposure to software and infrastructure tasks involving Python, Django, APIs and Eleventy.

Alongside this I build my own software projects and operate under the name Adalyn Technologies, an operating name rather than a registered company. It has its own website.

Engineering philosophy

  • A successful response is not proof of a successful outcome. Systems should verify what happened.
  • Data integrity belongs in the data model, not only in application code.
  • Who someone is and what they may do are separate questions.
  • Failures, retries and duplicates are normal. Design for them first.
  • Software that cannot be deployed, monitored and maintained is unfinished.

Technical interests

Distributed systems and consistency, API design, security and privacy engineering, geospatial systems, reliability engineering and digital public infrastructure.

Leadership and collaboration

I take responsibility for architecture and delivery decisions in my own projects, and I explain technical choices to non-technical stakeholders. I am open to collaborating with engineering teams and research partners.

Development and humanitarian technology

I am interested in technology for public-interest settings, where information is sensitive and mistakes have real consequences. I have not worked for a development or humanitarian organisation. My interest comes from the design problems in my own projects.

Direction

I want to work on reliable digital systems at an international level, deepen my practice in testing, security and observability, and publish original engineering analysis.
