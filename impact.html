Impact & Leadership

Reliability is how software earns trust.

Engineering and institutional reliability

Institutions depend on records being correct, payments being accounted for and access being controlled. When software fails in those places, the cost falls on people: a fee not credited, a record exposed, an alert that is wrong. That is why I treat payment states, authorization and failure handling as core design work.

Responsible data handling

Student records and missing-person reports are sensitive. Both call for collecting only what is needed, restricting who can see it, and being able to correct or withdraw it. I approach privacy as part of the data model, not a policy added afterwards.

What the two projects teach

  • School Management SaaS: multiple schools share one platform, so isolation, role-based access and trustworthy payment records are central design concerns.
  • Salama Kenya: a report is a managed lifecycle with verification, publication, correction and withdrawal, and decisions about what to reveal and to whom.

Leadership

My leadership so far is in owning technical decisions and communicating them clearly. I want to grow it in engineering teams and collaborations.

I do not claim measured social impact from either project, and I have not worked for an NGO, international institution or humanitarian agency. Claims on this site follow the evidence available for each project.
