What I pay attention to

Architecture

Clear module boundaries and the simplest structure that fits the requirement.

Databases

Relational models, constraints and transactions that protect data integrity.

APIs

Validation, consistent errors and permission checks on every route.

Authentication

JWT sessions with role-based access control, kept distinct from authorization.

Payments

Treat money as a state machine and verify before marking anything paid.

Reliability and deployment

Plan for retries and duplicates; configure and deploy deliberately.
