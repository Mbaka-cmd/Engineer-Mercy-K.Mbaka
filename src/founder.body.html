The problems I want to solve

Many organisations still run on paper registers, manual receipts and disconnected spreadsheets. They need software that fits how they actually work, handles money reliably and can be maintained by a small team. That is the kind of problem I am drawn to, and the reason I focus on workflows, data and payments rather than only interfaces.

Engineering and business value

A system earns its keep when it saves time, prevents errors and gives people confidence in their records. That is why I care about data integrity, permissions and payment verification: they are not technical decoration, they are what makes a system trustworthy to the person paying a fee or reconciling an account.

How I work

  • Start with the requirement. Understand the real workflow before choosing tools.
  • Choose the simplest architecture that works. Complexity needs a reason.
  • Integrate carefully. External services fail, retry and repeat themselves.
  • Deploy and support. Delivery includes configuration, troubleshooting and handover.
  • Explain clearly. Non-technical stakeholders should understand what they are getting.
