Beyond the Breakthrough

Understanding the technology. Examining the systems. Questioning the impact.

Research and original perspectives on the engineering systems behind emerging technology, and what it takes to make them useful, trustworthy, and relevant to the world we live in.

One story is published: The Day Mwende Paid Twice, a fictional scenario about payment callbacks. The rest are in development. Each article gets its own page, references and a clear separation of sourced facts from my own analysis.
